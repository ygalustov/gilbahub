Eleven of them (GH-349–GH-359) are unrelated bug-fixing and test-infrastructure work with no direct match to any of the 40 confirmed IDs in the Hoxton v6 audit; the remaining eleven (GH-360–GH-370) are audit-driven or directly uncovered while verifying audit-driven work, covering two defect clusters (D30 distributor/catalogue routing, D07a tissue gate) across three and five iterations respectively (D07a's chain grew from three to five entries in this update — GH-369 fixed a genuine contradiction the D07a tissue gate introduced, then needed its own same-day correction pass after independent review; GH-370 fixed a latent unit-conversion bug found while verifying D31), plus one grouped batch fix (GH-365). 2. **Tissue-lift-correction direction (Decision 1) — RESOLVED, implemented as GH-369.** Client decided: no, do not add a tissue-driven lift correction, and do not numerically reconcile the tissue-ratio removal figure with the tissue-sufficiency plant-status flag at all — the two stay independent measurements. Rationale: soil-side genuine undersupply already has its own correction mechanism (the floor-correction term, independent of the tissue ratio); a low tissue reading with sufficient soil more often indicates an uptake restriction (cation antagonism) than genuine undersupply, and forcing a lift correction in that case would reintroduce the over-application risk the D06/D07 fixes exist to prevent. Implemented instead as a structural fix: the plant-status flag now renders in the same row/unit as the tissue-ratio-derived figure, with an inline sentence explaining the two answer different questions, and the previously dose-less "apply foliar potassium immediately" advisory now states honestly that no generic foliar-K rate is verified in this codebase rather than inventing one.
